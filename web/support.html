Getting started

How do I send a message?

stonechat needs two iPhones, both running stonechat, with Bluetooth turned on and within Bluetooth range (roughly the same room). Open the app on both phones — they discover each other automatically, complete a secure handshake, and you can chat. There is no sign-up and no internet connection involved.

It says no devices found.

Check that Bluetooth is on for both phones, that both have stonechat open and have granted the Bluetooth permission, and that the phones are close together. For the most reliable connection, keep at least one phone in the foreground with the screen on.

Is it private?

Yes. Messages go directly between the two phones over Bluetooth, end-to-end encrypted. There are no servers and no accounts — nothing reaches us. See the Privacy Policy.

Does it work when the app is in the background?

Best-effort. Delivery is most reliable when at least one device is in the foreground with the screen on. Two backgrounded, screen-off phones may not find each other.

How do I stop talking to someone?

Connections are only to a device you’re in range of and choose to use. Close the app or move out of Bluetooth range to end a session.
