stonechat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

stonechat is offline, peer-to-peer messaging: two phones talk directly to each other over Bluetooth, with no servers and no internet. There is nothing for us to collect, because nothing ever reaches us.

The short version. No account. No servers. No internet for messaging. No analytics, no trackers, no third-party SDKs. Your messages go straight from your phone to the other person’s phone over Bluetooth, end-to-end encrypted, and are stored only on those two devices.

How messaging works

stonechat uses your device’s Bluetooth to discover and connect to another phone running stonechat that is physically nearby. Messages travel directly between the two devices — they never pass through a server or the internet.

What data we collect

None. stonechat has no backend and makes no network calls. We cannot see your messages, your contacts, or anything else, because none of it ever leaves the two devices in a conversation. On the App Store, stonechat’s privacy label is “Data Not Collected.”

Encryption

Messages are end-to-end encrypted in transit using standard cryptography (libsodium: Curve25519 key exchange, XSalsa20-Poly1305 for confidentiality, and Ed25519 signatures). Only the two paired devices can read a conversation. stonechat aims for casual, everyday privacy between two people who choose to connect; it does not promise protection against a determined forensic attacker.

What’s stored, and where

Your messages and the peers you’ve connected with are stored only on your device. You can delete the app to remove them. We never receive a copy.

Bluetooth permission

stonechat asks for Bluetooth so it can discover and exchange messages with a nearby device you choose to connect to. It is used for nothing else — no location, no advertising, no tracking.

Sharing & selling

We never share or sell your data, because we never have it.

Children

stonechat is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and the version published with the app.
